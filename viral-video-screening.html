Event Highlights: Viral Video Screening by Prashant Podagatlapalli

23 November 2025 · MC Joint Team
5 min read
Events · Premiere · Hyderabad

[ IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — Screening room at Ramanaidu Studios / audience photos / event atmosphere ]

Our journey with Prashant Podagatlapalli began with a pitch trailer. By our second meeting, he had already begun shooting. By the third, he had found a producer. And before long, he became part of the MC Joint family as a writer–director whose persistence and work ethic made him impossible to ignore.

"Viral Video was built with passion, persistence, and sleepless nights — and on 23 November 2025, an audience of 80 experienced it for the first time."

The Film: Viral Video

Viral Video was funded, shot, edited, and completed by Prashant with every resource he could gather. It's the kind of film that carries the energy of its making — you can feel the discipline and the sacrifice in it. It's also the kind of film that, when it finally reaches an audience, reminds everyone in the room why cinema matters.

[ IMAGE PLACEHOLDER — Film poster / key art for Viral Video ]

Viral Video — written and directed by Prashant Podagatlapalli, with music by Ravi Nidamarthy.

The Night: 23 November 2025, Ramanaidu Studios

All of Prashant's work came together on 23 November 2025, when MC Joint hosted the film's first official screening at Ramanaidu Studios, Hyderabad. The audience of 80 was carefully curated — people who would engage with the film seriously, offer real feedback, and represent the broader ecosystem of Telugu independent cinema.

The room was electric. Filmmakers, students, actors, faculty from LV Prasad Film School (Chennai), alumni from Ramanaidu Film School, and people from across age groups experienced the film together for the first time. The energy in the room was something every filmmaker — and every producer — should feel at least once.

This was the first official public step. What follows will be worth watching.
