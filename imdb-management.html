IMDb Profile Management

Your IMDb page is your industry CV. Make it count.

IMDb is the single most-referenced source in the film industry. Casting directors, producers, and journalists look you up there before anywhere else. A complete, accurate IMDb profile is not optional — it's infrastructure.

MC Joint handles the full scope: ensuring your credits are listed correctly, your headshot is professional, your biography is well-written, and your official links are connected.

What We Cover

  • Credit submission and correction for films, web series, short films and music
  • Headshot upload and primary photo management
  • Biography writing and editing
  • Official website and social media link setup
  • IMDbPro liaison support for represented talent
