About Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna

Kasala Narsanna — known widely as Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna — is a Telugu folk singer and balladeer from Nalgonda, Telangana. Drawing from the Gaddar tradition of socially conscious, people-rooted music, he has built a body of work that speaks directly to the soil and struggles of Telangana life.

With over 20 albums and 21 tracks, his catalogue spans folk anthems, devotional songs, political commentaries, and tribal folk traditions. Albums like Matti Vasana, Saani, Evvadidira Ee Bhoomi, and Maa Bhoomi Kosam Maa Nela Kosam reflect his commitment to singing about land, community, and belonging. His voice has found its way into cinema too — he is credited on the Dasara soundtrack.

With 131,000+ monthly listeners on Spotify and a Facebook community of over 94,000 fans, his reach extends well beyond Nalgonda. MC Joint represents Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna for live performances, studio collaborations, and music distribution.
