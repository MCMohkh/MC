About Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna

Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna is a folk singer based in Nalgonda, Telangana, who belongs to the tradition of jana padalu — Telugu folk songs historically used to express the concerns, aspirations, and political consciousness of common people. His music sits within a lineage that includes legendary figures such as Gaddar, the revolutionary folk singer whose work became central to Telangana's cultural and political movements.

His songs are rooted in Telangana's rural life, its struggles, and its distinct cultural identity — combining traditional musical forms with direct, emotionally resonant language. His performances carry the weight of lived experience and connect with audiences who rarely see their stories reflected in mainstream media.

MC Joint manages the music distribution for Nalgonda Gaddar Narsanna — placing his recordings on digital streaming platforms, handling metadata and artwork, and ensuring that folk and political music from Telangana reaches listeners across India and the diaspora.
