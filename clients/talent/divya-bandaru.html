About Divya Bandaru

Divya Bandaru is an actor, writer, and director working at the intersection of indie Telugu cinema and short-form storytelling. She co-wrote and directed Etthulu (2022) alongside Camp Sasi, and wrote and directed Pickpocket (2022) — a crime short that went on to garner enough attention to spawn a sequel.

As an actress, she has appeared in Ranganaayaki and Nirudyoga Natulu, where she played the role of The Landlord under the screen name Divya Bakshi. Her work spans both sides of the camera, reflecting a deep investment in storytelling at every level of production.
