Raj Tirandasu

From the stages of Avanti Cinema to the sets of Pushpa — Raj Tirandasu has built a career brick by brick, choosing every role with the conviction of someone who knows exactly where he is going.

About Raj Tirandasu

Raj Tirandasu was born in Nalgonda district, Telangana. He completed his MBA before making the uncommon decision to pursue acting — a choice that has paid off in a body of work that ranges from intimate indie web series to some of Telugu cinema's biggest theatrical productions.

His career began with Avanti Cinema — Rohit–Sasi cast him in Story Discussion, and later Anvesh Michael featured him as Nethi Rajesh in Kotha Poradu (AHA, 2020), a performance that earned him significant appreciation. Then Sukumar cast him as Mogileesu in Pushpa: The Rise (2021), and overnight, Raj Tirandasu had a national audience.

Since Pushpa, the phone hasn't stopped. He has appeared in Sita Ramam, 18 Pages, Butta Bomma, Bhagavanth Kesari, Michael, OG, Kattalan, and Thadayam (ZEE5 Tamil), steadily building a career across multiple industries and formats.

His Instagram shows 67,000+ followers who follow a working actor who simply loves the craft. MC Joint is proud to represent Raj Tirandasu for bookings, talent management, and project collaborations.
