About Raj Tirandasu

Raj Tirandasu is a Telugu actor with a growing body of work across mainstream and indie Telugu cinema. He is known for his work in Pushpa, Kotha Poradu, and his title role in Madhu Babu.

MC Joint handles his complete talent management — from audition coordination and brand endorsements to press and digital PR. He is one of the rising names in the Telugu industry currently represented by MC Joint.
