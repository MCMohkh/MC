About Faraz OG

Faraz OG is an independent rapper and a core member of B12 Band, represented by MC Joint. He is one of the artists we identified from the local Hyderabad cypher circuit — and he stood out immediately for the sharpness of his pen and the confidence of his delivery.

His single Zaruri Behes — released through MC Joint — is a showcase of what Faraz does best: layered lyricism, a distinctive voice, and a refusal to compromise his sound for easy palatability. He raps in a blend of Urdu, Hindi and English that reflects his background and gives his music a texture that's uniquely his own.

We work with Faraz on music releases, live performances, and strategic artist development within the Telugu and national independent hip-hop circuit.

B12 Band is Hyderabad's most tightly-knit hip-hop collective — a group of artists who came up through the city's cypher culture together. Faraz's role within the group goes beyond rapping: he is one of its creative anchors, contributing to the group's sonic identity as much as its lyrical output. His work through MC Joint includes music distribution, live bookings, and strategic positioning within the national independent rap circuit.
