About Rohit Penumatsa

Rohit Penumatsa is the director and creative force behind Avanti Cinema, one of Hyderabad's most consistent indie film production units. He has been in a close partnership with MC Joint since 2018.

Under his direction, Avanti Cinema has produced back-to-back theatrical releases — Double Engine, Sheeshmahal, and Chivaraku Migiledhi in 2024 alone. MC Joint manages music distribution, digital strategy, and screening event organisation for all Avanti Cinema projects.
