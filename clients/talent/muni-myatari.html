About Muni Myatari

Muni Myatari is a theatre-trained actor from Narayankhed, Telangana. He made his entry into films with Ram Gopal Varma’s Nagnam and has since built a diverse body of work spanning web series, feature films, and OTT originals.

He played the lead role of Auto Dany in the feature film Double Engine (directed by Rohit & Sasi, 2024), and portrayed Jollu Naveen in RGV’s Disha Encounter. His theatre training gives him a strong command of physical expression and character-driven performance.

MC Joint handles his talent representation, auditions, and booking management. He is currently based in Begumpet, Hyderabad and is available for film, OTT, and theatre projects.
