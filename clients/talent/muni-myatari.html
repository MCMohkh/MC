Muni Myatari

Theatre-trained Telugu actor known for raw, naturalistic performances. Lead in Double Engine; credits include RGV's Disha Encounter, AHA's Kotha Poradu, and more.

About Muni Myatari

Muni Myatari (also credited as Shanthi Muni / Shantha Muni) is a versatile Telugu actor with a strong theatre background, known for his raw, naturalistic performances across film and OTT content. Based in Hyderabad, represented by MC Joint for talent representation, auditions, and booking management.

He made his entry into films with Ram Gopal Varma's Nagnam and has since built a diverse body of work across web series, feature films, and OTT originals. He played the lead role of Auto Dany in Double Engine (2024, dir. Rohit & Sasi) and portrayed Jollu Naveen in RGV's Disha Encounter.
