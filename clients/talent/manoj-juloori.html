About Manoj Juloori

Manoj Juloori is a Telugu actor who has been building his career through the Telugu independent film circuit — a space that rewards consistency, preparation, and the ability to inhabit a character without calling attention to the act of acting. He has all three.

He brings a natural, unforced quality to the roles he plays. There are no tics, no borrowed mannerisms — just a grounded presence that makes the scene around him feel real. That kind of naturalism is exactly what the best directors in the current Telugu indie cycle are looking for, and it's why MC Joint represents Manoj for film, web series, and OTT projects.

He is currently being developed for character-driven roles across formats, with a focus on Avanti Cinema's extended network and the broader Telugu independent film ecosystem.
