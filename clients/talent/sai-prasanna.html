About Sai Prasanna

Sai Prasanna Kondra is a Telugu actress, writer, and creative producer whose breakthrough came with the critically acclaimed AHA web series Kotha Poradu (2020). Her portrayal of Sukanya — in what is widely regarded as one of the most authentic, culturally rooted Telugu web series of its era — announced her as an actress with serious instincts and real presence.

Kotha Poradu was a breakout for the entire Telangana indie circuit, and Sai Prasanna was one of the reasons it landed so hard. The show stripped away every layer of artifice and demanded genuine performance, and she delivered exactly that. Her work on the series resonated deeply across both Telugu states and opened doors to mainstream Telugu cinema.

Since then, she has steadily built her feature film credits — supporting roles in Venu Udugula's Virata Parvam (2022) alongside Rana Daggubati and Sai Pallavi, Bheemadevarapally Branchi (2023), and most recently W/O Anirvesh (2025). She has also appeared in Story Discussion (Season 1, 2021) — another Avanti Cinema project — which connects her directly to the MC Joint creative family.

Beyond acting, Sai Prasanna is a writer and associate producer actively involved in event management and curation. She founded @ayuevents — a reflection of the broader creative energy she brings to everything she touches. We represent her for acting projects, brand collaborations, and creative partnerships.
