Pickpocket

2022 Written & directed by Divya Bandaru
The Film

Pickpocket is a 2022 feature film written and directed by Divya Bandaru for Avanti Cinema.

MC Joint's Role

MC Joint was the marketing partner for Pickpocket, handling digital strategy and distribution for the film. The work sat alongside our long-running partnership with Avanti Cinema.

Music

The original soundtrack for the film was composed by Smaran of Wolfgang Studios. Soundtrack artwork, including the Thodu Dongalu cover, appears in the gallery below.
