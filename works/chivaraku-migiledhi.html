About the Project

Chivaraku Migiledhi is a 2024 Telugu feature film from the wider Avanti Cinema creative network, featuring actors from our talent roster including Anvesh Michael. As part of our ongoing music distribution partnership with Vivek Sagar's Tapeloop Records, MC Joint handled the global release of the film's original soundtrack.

Music distribution is more than just uploading files to a platform. For every release we handle, we ensure proper metadata, ISRC coding, label crediting, platform optimization, and post-release tracking — so the music reaches the right listeners and the right revenue flows back to the right people.

Our Role in Every Release

Every soundtrack MC Joint distributes goes through the same rigorous process: metadata review and tagging, ISRC and UPC assignment, label copy verification, platform-specific artwork optimisation, scheduled release coordination, and post-release streaming analytics. We treat independent cinema music with the same seriousness a major label would — because it deserves to be heard that way.

Part of a larger picture. Chivaraku Migiledhi is one of 12+ music releases MC Joint has distributed from the Avanti Cinema orbit — including Sheeshmahal, Nirudyoga Natulu, Pickpocket, Story Discussion, and Madhu Babu. See the full catalogue on the Avanti Cinema project page.
