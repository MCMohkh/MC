Gopi Galla
Goa Trip

15 days. 15 locations. A remote Gadwal village to the beaches of Goa. No permissions. Critically acclaimed as one of 2025's finest Telugu films.

Film Campaign Music Distribution Avanti Cinema 2025 Critically Acclaimed

The Film

Gopi Galla Goa Trip (November 2025) is directed by Rohit Penumatsa and co-directed by Camp Sasi. The story follows two young men named Gopi — lifelong friends from a village in Gadwal, Telangana — who chase a dream of seeing Goa together. It is a coming-of-age road film shot with documentary-like intimacy, grounded in the textures of real Telugu life.

The film was shot across 15 locations in 15 days — from Gadwal to the beaches of Goa — without any official shooting permissions. What it lacks in production infrastructure, it more than makes up for in emotional honesty, lived-in performances, and Ravi Nidamarthy's extraordinary score.

MC Joint's Role

MC Joint handled global music distribution for the Gopi Galla Goa Trip soundtrack — including the widely praised score by Ravi Nidamarthy and the hit single Nallani Gopayya. We also coordinated talent management and press for the MC Joint artists featured in the film, and supported the digital campaign through our extended network.
