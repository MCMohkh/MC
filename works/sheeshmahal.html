Sheeshmahal

A multilingual anthology film shot in 2013, released in theatres in 2024. Featuring Piyush Mishra and Rahul Ram of Indian Ocean. Music by Vivek Sagar — distributed by MC Joint.

Music Distribution Tapeloop Records Avanti Cinema Telugu · Hindi · English 2024

About the Project

Sheeshmahal is one of the most unusual films in recent Telugu indie cinema. An anthology of interconnected stories directed by Camp Sasi and edited by Rohit Penumatsa (as Avanti Ruya), it was shot across multiple languages — Telugu, Hindi, and English — in 2013. The film took over a decade to reach its theatrical release in February 2024, and when it did, it brought with it a remarkable cast and a singular musical identity.

The film features Piyush Mishra — the celebrated Bollywood actor and lyricist — alongside Rahul Ram, bassist and vocalist of the legendary Indian Ocean, and a full ensemble of Telugu independent cinema's finest actors. Vivek Sagar scored the film, bringing the same minimalist, textured approach that defines his best work.

MC Joint's Role

MC Joint, through our Tapeloop Records distribution partnership with Vivek Sagar, handled the global music distribution for the Sheeshmahal Original Motion Picture Soundtrack — placing it on Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, Resso, YouTube Music, and all major streaming platforms. We also supported the digital campaign around the theatrical release.
