About MC Joint

The MC Joint Story

MC Joint was founded in 2018 with a single conviction: that great stories deserve great distribution. We saw talented artists, filmmakers and creators in Hyderabad struggling not with their craft, but with reach, strategy, and representation.

Starting with social media management and small music distribution deals, we quickly grew into a full-service creative agency — one that handles everything from script development to brand sponsorships, from talent auditions to digital strategy reports for state governments.

Today, we are proud to have worked with acclaimed composers like Vivek Sagar, breakthrough cinema units like Avanti Cinema, India's first Muslim superhero comic Musalman Comix, and a growing roster of independent rap artists who are changing the game.

We don't position ourselves as vendors. We are partners — invested in your craft, your career, and your community.

The Team

Meet the Founders

Sonu Mohkh

Founder · Talent Agent · Comic Book Writer

A seasoned marketing strategist with expertise in digital branding, talent representation, and content marketing. Sonu has worked extensively with film production houses, OTT platforms, and brands to enhance their digital presence and audience engagement.

His vision for MC Joint was simple — build a creative home where artists are treated as partners, not products. He is also the creator behind original comic book IP developed under the MC Joint banner.

NJay

Co-Founder · Business Manager

NJay brings a strong foundation in account management and digital marketing to the team, with years of experience shaping brand communication and audience engagement across diverse digital channels.

Skilled in strategic planning, social media marketing, online advertising, and content development, he has played key roles managing client relationships and delivering results-driven campaigns.

Sai Charan

Co-Founder · Content Specialist

A content strategist and digital marketing expert, Sai Charan has successfully managed high-performance social media assets and led marketing campaigns for films like Sheeshmahal.

His work with Avanti Cinema and other media ventures highlights his ability to create impactful digital narratives that connect with audiences on a deep, cultural level.

Our Journey

Timeline

2018

MC Joint Founded

Started with social media management and early partnership with Avanti Cinema following the success of Nirudyoga Natulu.

2019

Music Distribution Begins

First official music distribution deals — distributing Avanti Cinema soundtracks across Spotify, Apple Music and more.

2020

Lockdown Innovation

Transformed Avanti Cinema's operations during COVID, pioneering new digital screening and community-release models.

2021

Talent Roster Expands

Signed Jagadeesh Prathap Bandari, Raj Tirandasu and several rising artists. Distributed Pickpocket OST by Smaran.

2024

Big Screen Presence

Back-to-back theatrical releases with Avanti Cinema — Double Engine, Sheeshmahal, Chivaraku Migiledhi.

2026

MC Joint Studios

Setting up in-house content studio. Expanding into original IPs — audio series, web shows, animated content.
