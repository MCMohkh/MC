Aarti Badruka's Circle

What this page is, and why it exists

What it is

A private guest and contact network for Aarti Badruka's PR and events work, built and hosted by MC. It replaces a scattered mix of spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and paper lists with one running list that Aarti's team can search, update and export from any device.

How people get added

Two ways: Aarti's team adds someone directly from the admin dashboard, or someone joins themselves through an invite link — shared over WhatsApp or email — that opens the short form this page hands off to. Self-joins sit as pending until the team approves them, so nothing is added to outreach lists without a human checking it first.

What it holds

  • Name, phone and (optionally) email
  • Address, pincode, area and a map pin or link, for planning invites geographically
  • The kinds of events someone is interested in — fashion, interiors, corporate and so on
  • A status per person — active, not in town, do not contact — so outreach always reflects who actually wants to hear from the team

Who can see what

The join form is public by design, so a link can be shared freely. The admin dashboard — where the full list lives — is locked behind a private key held only by Aarti's team. Details collected here are used solely for invites and outreach on Aarti's behalf, and are never sold or shared onward.
